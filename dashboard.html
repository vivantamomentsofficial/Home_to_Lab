Safe Usage Guidelines

To ensure your files and notes remain private on shared computer lab PCs, please follow these safety practices:

Tab Self-Destruct: Always close your browser tab when you leave the lab PC. This automatically wipes your active session and cookies.
Wipe Temporary Data: Delete your files manually after downloading them in the lab to keep your storage quota clean.
Zero Footprint: Files are stored behind secure Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Nobody else in the lab can view your files.
