Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about security, limits, or clipboard sync? We have answers.

How secure is CloudVault?

All your files and snippets are protected by Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Only your authenticated user account can access, download, or edit your private vault items. Lab computer logins are completely isolated.

Does it save sessions on shared lab PCs?

By default, if you don't check "Remember Me" during login, CloudVault terminates your session and wipes active cookies immediately upon tab or browser closure, ensuring nobody else can hijack your account after you leave the computer.

What is the storage file size limit?

CloudVault offers a generous storage size of up to 100MB per file upload on the standard student plan, which is perfect for programming scripts, assignments, ZIP archives, lab reports, and study guides.

Can I share files with my friends?

Yes! From your private dashboard, you can generate temporary share links for specific files. Anyone with the URL can access and download that file before it automatically expires and self-destructs from the database.
